FAQ

Questions brands ask.

How does pricing work?

Every campaign is scoped to you. You pay a management fee for us to run it end to end, and the creator budget is separate — so you always know exactly where your money goes. Tell us the goal and we'll send a tailored quote.

How fast can a campaign go live?

Most campaigns launch within about two weeks from brief to posting. Need it sooner? Some of our creators offer rush turnarounds.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No lock-in. Start with a single campaign and scale from there — we earn the next one by delivering on this one.

How do you pick the creator?

By fit, not just follower count — audience demographics, niche relevance, engagement and brand affinity. We only propose creators whose audience is genuinely right for you.

Can we use the content in our own ads?

Yes. We handle licensing per campaign, including UGC for paid social and whitelisting, so the best-performing content can run as ads on your own channels.

When do creators get paid?

Brands fund the campaign before it launches; we hold it in escrow and pay creators within 7 days of going live. No chasing, no delays.
