On the Roster

Join the
roster.

Applying to the Vantra roster is free. We're building a curated roster of micro and mid-tier creators — and when a brand brief fits your niche and audience, we put you forward. We don't chase deals on your behalf or promise a set number of campaigns; what we offer is a real shot at the briefs that genuinely fit you, run professionally and paid on time.

Curated by Fit, Not Follower Count

Built for Long-Term Relationships

The goal isn't one-off placements. We look to turn a good first campaign into recurring work with brands that genuinely fit you — better for the brand, and for you.
